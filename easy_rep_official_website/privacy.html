PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 2026

At Easy Rep, accessible from our official website and Android application, your privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Easy Rep and how we use and protect it.

01. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Easy Rep is built with a privacy-first architecture. Depending on how you use the application, we may collect or store the following:

  • Local Workout Data: Sets, reps, weights, workout history, and custom training splits created by you are stored directly and securely on your device's local storage (Offline Mode).
  • Account & Profile Information: If you choose to sign in using your Google Account (for cloud backups or competitive features), we collect basic profile details such as your display name and email address.
  • Leaderboard Data: Points and workout consistency metrics submitted when participating in the Easy Rep League weekly, monthly, or global leaderboards.

02. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect strictly to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Easy Rep application and its core tracking features.
  • Enable local data backup, export, and account restoration across sessions.
  • Calculate points and manage rankings within the Easy Rep League.
  • Respond to support inquiries, bug reports, or privacy requests.

03. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

Your core workout logs remain locally on your device unless you explicitly sign in and sync them. You can export or manage your workout backups at any time from the Profile & Data section inside the app. We apply industry-standard security practices to protect synced data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

04. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Easy Rep relies on trusted infrastructure providers (such as Google Authentication, Supabase for leaderboard and account synchronization, and Vercel for web hosting) strictly to deliver app functionality. These providers process data in accordance with strict security and privacy standards.

05. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as new features (such as Rival Week) are introduced. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
